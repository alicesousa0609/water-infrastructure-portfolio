# Project controls framework — water infrastructure (demonstration)

This portfolio translates real experience in construction coordination, field reporting, budgets, quantity measurements and subcontractor management into a **demonstration** water-network control framework.

| Gate | Deliverables | Primary management question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Define | Scope, WBS, stakeholder map | What must be delivered and accepted? |
| Plan | Schedule, resources, quantities, risk | What is the baseline and who is accountable? |
| Execute | Site coordination, supplier records, permits | Are constraints being resolved early? |
| Monitor | Accepted quantities, cost tracking, issues | Are we on plan and what changed? |
| Close | Punch list, test/inspection records, as-builts | Is the package traceable and handed over? |

## Measurement method
Track approved baseline metres, field-recorded installed metres, accepted metres, and remaining scope independently. Calculate accepted value only from validated quantities and clearly distinguish variations from baseline.

## Commercial and claims support
A defensible cost/claim narrative should identify scope, instructions, dates, evidence, quantified impact, and decision status. Supporting narrative does not itself prove a contractual entitlement.

## Stakeholder management
Coordinate drawing revisions, approvals, utilities, contractors, suppliers, site personnel and client representatives with one issue log and consistent status updates.
