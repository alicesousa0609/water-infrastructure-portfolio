# SANIT engineering case study — water-distribution networks
**Role:** Sanitation Engineering Intern · June 2017–June 2018
**Context:** Engineering support for urban water-supply networks associated with SABESP, São Paulo, Brazil.
**Scope of personal contribution:** CAD/GIS drawings and updates, supporting mapping/records, site engineering follow-up and technical documentation.

## Technical context
The work involved replacement of ageing iron pipelines with HDPE/PEAD alternatives, including nominal diameters of 100 mm and 150 mm where applicable. I was exposed to both conventional excavation and trenchless methods. Pipe sizes here are **diameters, not installed metres**; no installed quantity, cost saving or site management ownership is claimed.

## My contributions
- Prepared and updated drawings in AutoCAD, and worked with ArcGIS and available existing-services records.
- Supported technical mapping and coordination with the field engineering team.
- Accompanied selected field operations to understand works, interfaces and drawing changes.
- Supported technical reports, document registers and public/private utility information.
- Observed why inaccurate underground-services records require early risk escalation and field verification.
